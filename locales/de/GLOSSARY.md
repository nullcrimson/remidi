## Tone and forms

- Sie, as Windows, macOS and Google address a German user. Full sentences (tooltips, errors, documents) use Sie: "Prüfen Sie Ihre Verbindung", "Wählen Sie …".
- Buttons, short hints, placeholders and blockers use the infinitive, the standard German UI form: "Konvertieren & herunterladen", ".mid-Datei hinzufügen und beide Plugins wählen", "Zum Prüfen oder Zurücksetzen klicken".
- Short hints under an option say what happens to the missing drums: "werden durch das ähnlichste Instrument ersetzt", "werden aus der Datei entfernt".
- Register: friendly and plain, one musician to another. Prefer verbs to nouns ("Die Noten in der Datei bleiben, wie sie sind" not "Betrifft nur die Anzeige"), say errors in plain words ("Der Konverter kann diese Datei nicht lesen – sie sieht nicht nach MIDI aus"), and avoid office German and internal jargon (no "Drum-Vokabular", "Ersatzkette", "Slot" in user text; say "Ersatzinstrumente").
- Engine and brand names are never inflected or glued into compounds; phrasing uses "von X", "in X", "nach X", "X: …" ("Drum-MIDI von { $source } nach { $target } konvertieren", "Note in { $engine }").
- Quotes „…“; in-sentence dash is the spaced en dash (–); a leading "—" that English appends after a heading is kept as in English.
- Keyboard: Strg (German keyboard label for Ctrl).
- Plurals: German distinguishes one / other only; every count select has [one] and *[other], and English's [0] variants are kept.
- "Instrument" means a kit piece, so where English says "other instruments" (bass, keys) the German says "andere Spuren" to avoid the clash.

## English words kept

- Drum-MIDI, Drums (the drum part: "wenn Ihre Drums auf einem anderen Kanal liegen"), Drum-Kanal, Drum-Map, Drum-Plugin — German producers and drummers say "Drums", not "Schlagzeug", in a DAW context.
- Preset, Plugin, DAW (die DAW), Sound, Slot, Groove, Tool, Feedback, Upload — standard in German DAWs and music software.
- Ghost Notes, Rimshots, Crash, China, Hi-Hat, Tom — what German drummers say.
- GitHub-Issue — what German developers call it.
- FAQ, Auto — the usual German labels.
- Never translated: drum and family names, engine and product names, MIDI, Drumverter, note names (C1, D#2), file extensions (.mid, .zip, .json), GitHub, email addresses.
