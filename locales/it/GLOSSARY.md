## Tone and forms

Informal "tu", as in Apple and Google Italian UIs, with bare imperatives on buttons and in instructions ("Scegli", "Aggiungi", "Fai clic", "Controlla la connessione e riprova"). Errors use the standard impersonal "Impossibile …" (Windows, macOS, Google). Confirmation questions use the infinitive ("Eliminare i preset salvati…?").

Short hint text under an option describes what the option does, third person ("li suona sullo strumento più vicino", "li scarta"), matching the tooltip.

Counted words agree in gender and number with the implied noun, so number-only chips get a one/other select even where English has none ("1 approssimata" / "3 approssimate"). "File", "plugin" and "preset" are invariable, so "3 file", "80 plugin", "2 preset".

Engine and brand names are never inflected: "Converti X in Y" (never "a"/"ad" before a name), "Nota X", "Mappa delle note MIDI di X", "l'origine su GetGood Drums e la destinazione su EZdrummer". Quotes around user names use “ ”.

## English words kept

- **plugin, preset, DAW, editor, report, feedback, slot, groove, browser, account, cookie, email, app**: standard in Italian software and among Italian producers; the Italian alternatives are unused or sound bureaucratic.
- **ghost note, rimshot, crash, China, hi-hat, tom**: what Italian drummers say. Loanwords are invariable in Italian, so "le ghost note", "i rimshot".
- **FAQ**: the usual Italian label for a FAQ page; the heading uses "Domande frequenti".
- **Auto**: the usual Italian short label for an automatic setting (camera and audio apps).
- **signature** (MixWave "kit signature"): the term Italian drummers use for an artist-branded kit.
